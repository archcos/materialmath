How to calculate concrete

Pick the shape in the calculator: a slab, patio or footing, a round slab, or a set of steps. For fence posts and Sonotube columns, use the post hole concrete calculator.

A slab or footing is a rectangular box, so the amount of concrete is simply its volume. Measure everything in the same unit first. In feet, that means turning the thickness from inches into feet by dividing by 12.

Slab or footing (cu ft) = length (ft) × width (ft) × thickness (in) ÷ 12
Round slab = 3.1416 × (diameter ÷ 2)² × thickness
Steps = width × tread × rise × n(n + 1) ÷ 2, for n steps
Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27

Solid steps are a stack of blocks: the bottom step is one riser tall, the next is two, and so on. That is where n(n + 1) ÷ 2 comes from. A landing at the top can be added as a separate slab.

Ready-mix concrete is sold by the cubic yard (or cubic metre). Premix bags are sold by weight, and the bag label tells you how much volume it makes. A typical 80 lb bag yields about 0.6 cu ft, a 60 lb bag about 0.45 cu ft and a 40 lb bag about 0.3 cu ft.

Worked example

A 10 × 10 ft patio, 4 inches thick:

  • 10 × 10 × (4 ÷ 12) = 33.3 cu ft
  • Add 10% for waste: 33.3 × 1.10 = 36.7 cu ft, or about 1.36 cu yd
  • 80 lb bags: 36.7 ÷ 0.6 = 61.1, so buy 62 bags

That is a lot of bags to mix by hand. Once a job needs more than about one cubic yard, a ready-mix delivery or a rented mixer usually saves time and often money.

Ordering ready-mix

Ready-mix plants usually sell in steps of ¼ or ½ cubic yard (or ¼ m³), so the calculator rounds your volume up to the next ¼. A full US mixer truck carries about 9–10 cubic yards, and a UK truck about 6–8 m³. Small loads often have a short-load fee, so ask for the full delivered price.

Tips for ordering the right amount

  • Measure depth in several places. Ground is rarely flat. Average your measurements, because an extra half inch across a slab adds up fast.
  • Never order exactly. Running short mid-pour leaves a weak cold joint. Spillage, form bulges and an uneven base all eat concrete, so keep 5–10% extra.
  • Check the bag. Yields vary slightly by brand and mix type, so confirm the yield printed on the bag you buy.

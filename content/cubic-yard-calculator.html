How to calculate cubic yards

A cubic yard is a cube 3 ft on each side, which is 27 cubic feet. To find the cubic yards in a flat area, multiply length, width and depth in feet, then divide by 27.

Cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12 ÷ 27
Tons = cubic yards × weight per cubic yard

For example, 10 × 10 ft at 4 in deep is 33.3 cu ft, which is 1.23 cubic yards. In metric, multiply metres to get cubic metres: 1 m³ is about 1.31 cubic yards.

Yards to tons

Each material weighs a different amount, so a ton of mulch fills far more space than a ton of gravel. Typical dry weights:

Wet material can weigh 20–30% more. Suppliers who sell by the ton may use their own weights, so ask how many yards their ton fills.

Three ways to use the calculator

  • The size of the area: enter length, width and depth to get cubic yards and tons.
  • The volume: enter cubic yards (or m³) to convert to tons.
  • The weight: enter tons (or tonnes) to convert to cubic yards.

Worked example

A supplier sells crushed stone at 10 tons per truck. How many yards is that?

  • Crushed stone weighs about 1.4 tons per cubic yard
  • 10 ÷ 1.4 = 7.1 cubic yards
  • At 4 in deep, 7.1 yards covers about 7.1 × 81 = 575 sq ft

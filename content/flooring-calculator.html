How to calculate flooring

Laminate, vinyl plank (LVP) and engineered or solid hardwood are sold in boxes that each cover a fixed area. Measure the room, add extra for cuts, then divide by the box coverage printed on the label.

Flooring to buy = length × width × (1 + extra %)
Boxes = flooring to buy ÷ coverage per box (round up)

Worked example

A 12 × 15 ft living room with laminate that covers 20 sq ft per box:

  • Floor area: 12 × 15 = 180 sq ft
  • With 10% extra: 198 sq ft
  • Boxes: 198 ÷ 20 = 9.9, so buy 10 boxes

How much extra to add

  • 5–10% for a simple rectangular room with planks laid straight.
  • 10–15% for rooms with many corners, closets, or a diagonal layout.
  • 15–20% for herringbone and chevron patterns.

Measuring tips

  • Measure to the walls, not to the baseboards. The expansion gap is hidden under the trim, and that small amount is covered by your extra.
  • Include closets and the space under appliances that will sit on the new floor.
  • L-shaped rooms: split the room into two rectangles, calculate each, and add them together.

Before you install

  • Many manufacturers ask you to let the flooring acclimate in the room for a day or two before installing. Follow the instructions in the box.
  • Check whether you also need underlayment. Some products have it attached, others need a separate roll, sold by area.
  • Keep one unopened box for repairs. It is often impossible to match the color later.

How to calculate studs for a wall

Divide the wall length by the stud spacing and add one for the end. Then add extra studs where the wall needs them: corners, places where other walls meet, and the sides of doors and windows.

Studs = wall length ÷ spacing + 1 + 2 per corner + 2 per opening
Plate length = wall length × number of plates

A wall has one bottom plate and either one or two top plates. Load-bearing walls usually get a double top plate, so 3 plates in total.

Worked example

A 12 ft wall with studs at 16 in on center, 2 corners, 1 window and a double top plate:

  • Studs along the wall: 144 in ÷ 16 = 9, plus 1 = 10
  • Corners: 2 × 2 = 4 more. Window: 2 more. Total 16
  • Add 10% for crooked boards and mistakes: 18 studs
  • Plates: 12 ft × 3 = 36 ft. With 10% extra, that is three 16 ft boards

What this calculator leaves out

  • Headers: each door and window needs a header across the top, sized to the opening width and the load.
  • Jack and cripple studs: wide openings need jack studs under the header and short cripple studs above and below the window. Add them to the stud count.
  • Blocking: fire blocking, and backing for cabinets or grab bars, comes out of the extra allowance or should be added.

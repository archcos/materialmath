How to calculate gravel

Gravel is sold by volume (cubic yards or cubic metres) or by weight (tons or tonnes). First find the volume of the area you want to fill, then convert it to weight using the density of the stone.

Volume (cu ft) = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12
Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27
Weight ≈ cubic yards × 1.4 tons

This calculator uses 1.68 tonnes per cubic metre (about 1.4 US tons per cubic yard), a typical figure for crushed stone and pea gravel. Very fine or wet material weighs more, while light stone such as lava rock weighs much less.

Worked example

A path area of 10 × 10 feet, 3 inches deep:

  • 10 × 10 × (3 ÷ 12) = 25 cu ft
  • Add 5% for settling: 26.25 cu ft = 0.97 cu yd
  • Weight: about 1.4 tons

How deep should gravel be?

UseTypical depth
Decorative ground cover2 in (50 mm)
Garden paths2–3 in (50–75 mm)
Base under pavers or a shed4–6 in (100–150 mm), compacted
Driveway (base and top layer)8–12 in (200–300 mm) in layers

Tips

  • Compacted bases need extra. Crushed stone shrinks when you compact it, so add 10–15% instead of 5% for a compacted base.
  • Ask the supplier for their density. If they sell by the ton, ask how many tons make a cubic yard of that product and adjust.
  • Use landscape fabric under decorative gravel to slow weeds and stop the stone sinking into the soil.

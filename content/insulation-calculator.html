How to calculate insulation

Find the area you are insulating, then divide it by the area one bag covers at your target R-value. Every bag has a coverage chart, because the same bag covers less area when you lay it thicker for a higher R-value.

Area = length × width
Bags = area ÷ coverage per bag (at your R-value)
Thickness = R-value ÷ R per inch

For walls, enter the total wall length and the wall height. You do not need to take out the studs, because batts are sized to fit between them and the bag coverage already allows for the framing.

Products vary, so go by the thickness on your bag. In metric, R-value is RSI: divide the US R-value by 5.68. R-38 is about RSI 6.7.

Worked example

A 30 × 20 ft attic floor topped up to R-38 with blown-in cellulose, where the bag chart says 40 sq ft per bag at R-38:

  • Area: 30 × 20 = 600 sq ft
  • Bags: 600 × 1.05 ÷ 40 = 15.75, so 16 bags
  • Depth: 38 ÷ 3.6 = about 10½ in

Tips

  • Seal air leaks first. Caulk and foam gaps around pipes, wires and light fixtures before adding insulation.
  • Keep vents clear. Use baffles at the eaves so insulation does not block the soffit vents.
  • Mark the depth. Staple rulers to a few joists before blowing in insulation, so you can see when you reach the right depth.

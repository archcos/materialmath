How to calculate roof area

You can measure the roof safely from the ground. Measure the footprint of the house including the overhangs (eaves), then multiply by a pitch factor that accounts for the slope. A steeper roof has more surface over the same footprint.

Pitch factor = √(1 + (rise ÷ 12)²)
Roof area = footprint length × footprint width × pitch factor
Squares = roof area ÷ 100
Bundles = squares × 3

Roofers measure in squares: one square is 100 sq ft of roof. Most asphalt shingles come three bundles to a square, but check the wrapper, because some heavier shingles need four or more.

Worked example

A house with a 40 × 30 ft footprint including overhangs, with a 6/12 pitch:

  • Footprint: 40 × 30 = 1,200 sq ft
  • Roof area: 1,200 × 1.118 = 1,342 sq ft
  • With 10% extra: 1,476 sq ft = 14.8 squares
  • Bundles: 14.8 × 3 = 44.3, so buy 45 bundles

How much extra to add

  • 10% for a simple gable roof.
  • 15% or more for hip roofs, valleys, dormers and other cut-up roofs.

Other materials to budget for

  • Starter strip shingles along the eaves and rakes
  • Hip and ridge cap shingles, measured by the length of the hips and ridges
  • Underlayment (felt or synthetic), sold by rolls of a stated area
  • Drip edge, flashing and roofing nails

Roof work at height is dangerous. If you are not trained and equipped for it, use this estimate to check contractor quotes rather than to do the job yourself.

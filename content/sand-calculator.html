How to calculate sand

Sand fills a flat layer, so you need its volume: area times depth. Bulk sand is sold by the cubic yard or ton (m³ or tonne), and bagged sand by the cubic foot or kilogram.

Volume (cu ft) = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12
Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27
Tons ≈ cubic yards × 1.35

The calculator uses about 1.6 tonnes per cubic metre (1.35 US tons per cubic yard), typical for dry sand. Wet sand weighs more.

Worked example

A 10 × 10 ft patio base with 2 in of sand:

  • 10 × 10 × (2 ÷ 12) = 16.7 cu ft
  • Add 5% for settling: 17.5 cu ft = 0.65 cubic yards
  • Weight: about 0.9 tons
  • In bags: 17.5 ÷ 0.5 = 35 bags of 0.5 cu ft (50 lb)

Bags or bulk?

  • One cubic yard is 54 bags of 0.5 cu ft. One cubic metre is about 64 bags of 25 kg.
  • Above about half a yard, bulk delivery is usually cheaper than bags, even with a delivery fee.

How to calculate siding

Work out the area of the walls: the distance around the house times the wall height. Add the triangles on the gable ends, take out doors and windows, and add extra for cuts and overlaps.

Walls = 2 × (length + width) × wall height
Gables = width × gable height (two triangles)
Squares = (walls + gables − openings) × (1 + extra) ÷ 100

Siding is sold by the square, which is 100 sq ft of wall. Vinyl usually comes in boxes of 2 squares.

Worked example

A 40 × 30 ft house with 9 ft walls, 6 ft gables on both short ends, 2 doors and 8 windows:

  • Walls: 2 × (40 + 30) × 9 = 1,260 sq ft
  • Gables: 30 × 6 = 180 sq ft
  • Openings: 2 × 20 + 8 × 15 = 160 sq ft
  • Area: 1,260 + 180 − 160 = 1,280 sq ft
  • With 10% extra: 1,408 sq ft = 14.1 squares, or 8 boxes of vinyl

Don't forget the trim

  • Corner posts: one for each outside and inside corner, as tall as the wall.
  • J-channel: around every window and door, and along the roof line on the gables.
  • Starter strip: along the bottom of every wall, the same length as the distance around the house.
  • Soffit and fascia: under the eaves, if they are being replaced too.

How to calculate square footage

Square footage is the area of a space in square feet. For a rectangle, multiply the length by the width, both in feet.

Square feet = length (ft) × width (ft)
Square yards = square feet ÷ 9
Square metres = square feet ÷ 10.764

If you measured in feet and inches, turn the inches into a decimal first by dividing them by 12. For example, 12 ft 6 in is 12.5 ft.

Worked example

A room 12 feet wide and 15 feet long:

  • Area: 12 × 15 = 180 sq ft
  • In square yards: 180 ÷ 9 = 20 sq yd
  • In square metres: 180 ÷ 10.764 = 16.7 m²
  • Perimeter: 2 × (12 + 15) = 54 ft

Measuring other shapes

Most rooms and yards can be broken into simple shapes. Work out each piece and add them together:

ShapeArea
Rectangle or squarelength × width
Trianglebase × height ÷ 2
Circle3.1416 × radius × radius
L-shaped roomsplit into two rectangles

For an L-shaped room 20 ft long, where one part is 12 ft wide and a 10 ft section is only 8 ft wide: one rectangle is 10 × 12 = 120 sq ft, the other 10 × 8 = 80 sq ft, so the total is 200 sq ft.

What square footage is used for

  • Flooring and tile: buy by area, plus extra. See the flooring and tile calculators.
  • Paint: uses wall area instead. See the paint calculator.
  • Carpet is often priced by the square yard, so divide by 9.

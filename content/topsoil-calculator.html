How to calculate topsoil

Topsoil fills a flat layer, so you need the volume of that layer: area times depth. Bulk soil is sold by the cubic yard or cubic metre, and bagged soil by the cubic foot or litre.

Volume (cu ft) = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12
Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27

For weight, this calculator uses about 1.3 tonnes per cubic metre (roughly 1.1 US tons per cubic yard), typical for dry to slightly moist topsoil. Wet soil can weigh noticeably more.

Worked example

A new lawn area of 10 × 10 feet with 4 inches of topsoil:

  • 10 × 10 × (4 ÷ 12) = 33.3 cu ft
  • Add 5% for settling: 35 cu ft = 1.30 cu yd
  • Weight: about 1.4 tons

How much topsoil depth do you need?

ProjectTypical depth
Top-dressing an existing lawn¼–½ in (6–12 mm)
Filling low spots1–2 in (25–50 mm) at a time
New lawn from seed or sod4–6 in (100–150 mm)
Flower beds6–8 in (150–200 mm)
Raised vegetable beds8–12 in (200–300 mm)

Tips

  • Soil settles. Freshly spread soil sinks as it settles and gets watered. Raised beds can lose an inch or two in the first season, so fill them a little high.
  • Bulk or bags? For anything over about one cubic yard, bulk delivery is usually far cheaper than bags. Check how close the truck can get and whether you need to move it by wheelbarrow.
  • Raised beds: for an irregular bed, split it into rectangles, calculate each one and add them up.
